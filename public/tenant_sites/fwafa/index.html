FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Dry stone walling is a traditional building technique where stones are stacked without mortar or cement. The walls are held together by the weight of the stones and the skill of the craftsman in selecting and placing each stone. This method has been used for thousands of years and creates durable, breathable structures that can last for centuries.

When properly constructed, dry stone walls can last for hundreds of years. Many walls built centuries ago are still standing today. The key to longevity is skilled craftsmanship, quality stone selection, and proper foundation work. Regular maintenance can extend the life of a wall even further.

We are based in the Cotswolds and serve the entire Cotswolds region and surrounding areas. For larger projects, we're happy to travel further. Please get in touch with details of your project location, and we'll be happy to discuss your requirements.

We work with a variety of natural stones depending on your location, preference, and the project requirements. Common options include limestone, sandstone, granite, and local field stone. We always recommend using stone that's native to your area for the most authentic appearance and best integration with the landscape.

Our drone services include site surveys, aerial photography and videography, progress documentation, 3D mapping and modeling, and topographical assessments. This technology allows us to plan projects more effectively, identify potential challenges, and provide you with stunning visual records of the work.
