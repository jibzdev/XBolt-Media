WHAT WE OFFER

Aerial Solutions

Site Surveys & Mapping

Comprehensive aerial surveys providing detailed topographical data, boundary mapping, and volumetric calculations for construction and development projects.

  • Topographical surveys with contour mapping
  • Volumetric analysis for earthworks
  • Boundary and land measurement
  • GIS-ready data exports

Aerial Photography & Video

Professional 4K aerial photography and videography for marketing, documentation, and promotional purposes. Stunning perspectives that showcase your property or project.

  • 4K Ultra HD video capture
  • High-resolution photography (20MP+)
  • Professional editing & color grading
  • Real estate marketing packages

3D Modeling & Orthomosaic

Advanced photogrammetry creating detailed 3D models and orthomosaic maps. Perfect for precise planning, design visualization, and progress monitoring.

  • 3D point cloud generation
  • Orthomosaic map creation
  • Digital terrain models (DTM)
  • CAD/BIM compatible exports

Construction Monitoring

Regular aerial documentation of construction progress. Track developments, identify issues early, and create comprehensive visual records for stakeholders.

  • Weekly/monthly site visits
  • Progress comparison overlays
  • Time-lapse video creation
  • Stakeholder reports & updates
